About Sibu Kamek

The next chapter should sound like Sibu.

In July 2026, the Sibu Economic Zone was announced with an initial allocation of RM1.5 billion under the Sarawakku Sayang 2026 programme. Sibu Kamek complements that vision with a citizen-led envisioning exercise.

The transformation of Sibu is expected to take fifteen to twenty years. This planning window is when public participation carries the most weight: residents' voices should be written into those plans before they are finished, not after.

The name carries the idea

Kamek

Or "My Sibu". Sibu Kamek invites individual visions from markets, schools and longhouses to contribute to one shared vision, rooted in local voices rather than imported ideas.

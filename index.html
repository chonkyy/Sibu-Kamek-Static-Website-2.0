ABOUT SIBU KAMEK

The next chapter should sound like Sibu.

In July 2026, the Right Honourable Datuk Patinggi Tan Sri (Dr) Abang Haji Abdul Rahman Zohari bin Tun Datuk Abang Haji Openg, the Premier of Sarawak, announced the establishment of the Sibu Economic Zone (SEZ), backed by an initial allocation of RM1.5 billion under the Sarawakku Sayang 2026 programme for the Sibu Division. The state has set a clear vision for the zone. Sibu Kamek complements it with a citizen-led envisioning exercise, so that when the SEZ agency is formed its priorities already carry a mandate from the people of Sibu.

The transformation of Sibu is expected to take fifteen to twenty years, with roughly five years of planning before implementation proceeds in stages. That planning window is precisely when public participation carries the most weight. Sibu Kamek is a citizen-led effort to make sure residents' voices are written into those plans before they are finished, not after.

Programmes · Wishing Trees

What do you wish for Sibu?

Tree-shaped boards invite everyone to post, in any language, what they wish for Sibu. The trunk asks what development means. The fruits hold the future people want. The roots name what must be kept.

Harvested weekly and paired with a digital twin, the walls turn everyday hopes into the questions that shape the “My Sibu” survey.

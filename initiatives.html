Initiatives · The core events

From many voices
to one vision.

The six major events being conducted to engage the community in shaping Sibu's future.

01

2026

Stakeholder mapping

Discussions and exploration to understand who should be engaged in Sibu's future.

02

2026

Wishing trees

Tree-shaped boards in public spaces where anyone writes what they wish for Sibu.

03

2026

Surveys and media

A survey for citizens and diaspora in multiple languages, carried by paper, digital, radio and social media.

04

2026 onwards

Future workshops

Sessions for children, youth and elders to explore Sibu's possible futures before making choices.

05

2026 onwards

Public dashboard

Everything Sibu said, shown openly online so anyone can see it and respond.

06

2026 onwards

Seat for the Rajang

The river and the forest given a voice in decisions that will change them.

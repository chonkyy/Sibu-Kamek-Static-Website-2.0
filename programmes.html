Programmes · Wishing trees

What do you wish for Sibu?

Tree-shaped boards invite everyone to post, in any language, what they wish for Sibu. The trunk asks what development means. The fruits hold the future people want. The roots name what must be kept.

Harvested weekly and paired with a digital twin, the walls turn everyday hopes into the questions that shape the My Sibu survey.
